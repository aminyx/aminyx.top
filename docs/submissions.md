## 8. three.js forum — Showcase post (~150 words)

**Title:** aminyx.top — portfolio built on a live multipath-network simulation (R3F)

I rebuilt my portfolio around a full-page simulation of the multipath networking platform I'm building (Aminyx Link). The substrate is React Three Fiber with custom clip-space shaders on Points/LineSegments. The sim is plain JS: 176 nodes (84 on mobile), k=3 nearest-neighbor edges, per-edge heat, packets hopping edge to edge, failover cascades when a region dies. Visitors interact: a click kills a node into 10 ballistic shards, a long press starts a storm, a hidden terminal (`) drives kill/heal/storm/chaos, and the Konami code triggers CHAOS MODE. One EffectComposer with mipmapBlur Bloom (luminanceThreshold 0.55) that breathes with the cascades. Drei's PerformanceMonitor drops DPR to 1 and cuts postprocessing on weak GPUs (with hysteresis); DPR is capped at 1.75; `frameloop="never"` plus a static frame under prefers-reduced-motion; canvas2d fallback for no-WebGL2 and Save-Data. The three chunk (~319 KB gzip) lazy-loads after first paint.

https://aminyx.top
